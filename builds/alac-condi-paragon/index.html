Notes

AAA stands for Auto-Attack Alacrity.

This is a build which uses mostly Vipers Gear to maximise the dual swords new reworked bleeding capabilities.

This build provides sizeable group barrier and frequent applications of group stability as part of its rotation.

Rotation

Opener

  1. Signets (Cast very early to maintain 5 stacks)
  2. Chant of Action
  • None of these skills will trigger the boss fight, and you can perpetually keep refreshing signet until the fight begins.
  • Signet of Fury and "To the Limit!" provide adrenaline so you may use them in between fight phases or as part of your opener

DPS Rotation

Use the following

  1. Bloodthirster (⚔ Burst)
  2. Impale (⚔ 4)
  3. Rend (⚔ 3)
  4. Dolyak Signet
  5. Signet of Fury (when you need Adrenaline)
  6. Use skills on cooldown/when required.
  • Try to maintain 5 stacks of Signet Mastery.
  • Dolyak Signet has no cast time.
  • Try to queue skills with cast time to reduce interruption of autos.
  • A well timed Riposte (⚔5) increases damage significantly on actual fights.

Alacrity Rotation

  1. Chant of Action
  2. Double cast your chants when you have full adrenaline:
    • Chant of Recuperation for barrier
    • Chant of Freedom for stability
    • Return to Chant of Action to upkeep Strengthening Stanzas
  3. If you need extra adrenaline, use Signet of Fury

Longbow

Longbow provides additional ranged damage uptime and CC options.

Sustain in Open World

  1. Healing Signet for baseline healing
  2. Dolyak Signet for stun break, healing and stability.
  3. Chant of Recuperation for barrier and healing
  4. Chant of Freedom for stability
  5. Riposte for block.
  6. Use "We Will Never Yield!" in emergencies
  7. Use Relic of the Biomancer for extra baseline healing
  8. Use Relic of the Mistfire for life siphon
