This is the condition variant of an old but popular build without weapon swaps. The ranged nature of the build allows higher damage uptime and a full set of Ritualist's gear improves survivability. This build lacks self-boons for solo play.

Although the rotation is easy to learn, energy management requires active participation and requires players to to swap legends during a narrow window.

Rotation

Precast/Opener

  1. Legendary Renegade Stance
    • Precast Soulcleave's Summit
  2. First opener
    • Kalla: Icerazor's Ire -> Razorclaw's Rage
    • Generate Alacrity -> DPS Rotation Loop

Generating Alacrity

  • Orders from Above on CD

DPS Rotation Loop

After Opener:

  1. Use Weapon Skills on cooldown +/- Fillers
    • Bloodbane Path (S.Bow 2)
    • Sevenshot (S.Bow 3)
    • Spiritcrush (S.Bow 4)
  2. Where required, burn Remaining Energy
  3. Legend Swap @ Energy < 10%
    • Legendary Renegade Stance OR
    • Legendary Demon Stance
  4. Start with opener
    • Mallyx: Embrace the Darkness OR
    • Kalla: Icerazor's Ire -> Razorclaw's Rage
  5. Repeat Loop

Mallyx Fillers

  • Embrace the Darkness has an ICD of 3s. Spread out your bow skills with Auto-Attack Fillers to maximise its effects.
    • You will be energy starved during Mallyx.
    • If you need energy for CC, use autos during the Fillers.

Kalla Energy Burn

  • It is better to burn your energy than to reserve it due to Charged Mists.
    • You will have abundant energy during Kalla.
    • Try to time your CC phases during this stance
    • Burn excess energy using Citadel Bombardment
    • As a last resort, use Soulcleave's Summit if you can't Legend Swap fast enough.

Optimisation

CC

  1. Scorchrazor (Short Bow 5)
  2. Call to Anguish is available in Mallyx
  3. Darkrazor's Daring is available in Kalla
  4. Abyssal Blot (Spear 4 w.swap)
  5. Temporal Rift (Axe 5 w.swap)
  6. You may bring a staff if you really need a strong CC

Others

  1. Banish Enchantment for boon strip
  2. Use Unyielding Impact (flip skill) after CC for damage and energy burn
