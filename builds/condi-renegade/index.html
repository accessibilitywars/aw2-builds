This is a build for condi renegade, without using charged mists to make it easier to learn and use in content. The goal of this build is to avoid charged mists, so that players will not have to actively pay attention to energy management, except when it demon stance as it continuously drains energy. This is a great build for those looking to get into content like raids, and does good DPS alongside its low easier rotation (but still higher APM than most AW builds). This build has both a melee weapon set and a ranged weapon set, which means that you can use ranged as necessary in content. This does not actively provide boons as it is a selfish DPS build. The benchmark for this build is 36k with normal food and utility, but with ascended food, a writ for utility, and infusions it can reach just over 37k.

Rotation

Opener

You will start on Kalla/ Legendary Renegade Stance and with Short Bow.

  1. Icerazor's Ire
  2. Razorclaw's Rage
  3. Sevenshot (Short Bow 3)

DPS Rotation Loop

After Opener and demon + Maxe (mace+axe) loop:

  1. Stance swap immediately following the Short Bow 3 from the opener to: Mallyx/ Legendary Demon Stance
  2. Activate Embrace the Darkness
  3. Weapon swap to Maxe (mace+axe)
    • Searing Fissure (Mace 2)
    • Echoing Eruption (Mace 3)
    • Temporal Rift (Axe 5)
  4. Use the skills from steps 3 off cool down until you have run out of energy or just enough that you cannot cast another Searing Fissure
  5. Stance swap to Kalla/ Legendary Renegade Stance
    • Searing Fissure (Mace 2)
  6. Weapon swap to Short Bow.
    • Spiritcrush (Short Bow 4)
    • Sevenshot (Short Bow 3)
    • Bloodbane Path (Short Bow 2)
    • Icerazor's Ire
    • Razorclaw's Rage
  7. Use the skills from step 6 off cool down until you can stance swap to Mallyx/ Legendary Demon Stance (Cont. after step 1)

Optimisation

DPS increase

Gaining more DPS is done by performing the rotation faster, raising it from the initial 60 APM to 70 by manually pressing your autoattack skill after Short Bow2 and mace 2 as it will cancel the long animation (as long as your castbar has finished). The second method is related to gear, as you can equip infusions in ascended/legendary items  Malign Infusion, in addition to using the best food and utility.

CC

  1. Scorchrazor (Short Bow 5)
  2. Call to Anguish in Mallyx/Demon stance
  3. Darkrazor's Daring in Kalla/Renegade stance
  4. Temporal Rift (Axe 5)

Raid Ready

  1. Wing 1
    • For Vale Guardian, mace 2 may cause you to be teleported it used during the teleport mechanic. Thus, you will want to quickly step out of it to avoid the teleport.
    • Sabetha's flamewall is an instakill mechanic, however, mace 2 will not take you into her hitbox so you can safely use it during the flamewall if you are behind her or to her side.
  2. Wing 2
    • For Sloth, be aware that you may mistakenly pull a friendly slubling with axe 5 so be aware of where it is pointed.
  3. Wing 3
    • For Xera's pre-event, you can easily take a button and use your Short Bow to destroy the crystal on the second platform.
  4. Wing 4
    • For Deimos it is important to remember that your mace 2 may take you into an oil, so be very cautious of when you use it or to omit it entirely when oils spawn.
  5. Wing 5
    • For Soulless Horror you must be warry of Icerazor's Ire as it can immobilize the tormented dead, and cause trouble for your pusher.
    • For Dhuum, in the final 10% you must be outside of his hitbox but within the dome for several of your skills to hit the boss.
  6. Wing 6
    • For Twin Largos, you can begin the fight and most platform transitions using the opener and if you have an excess amount of energy then using Citadel Bombardment and/or Scorchrazor.
  7. Wing 7
    • For Adina CM, Frigid Blitz will let you jump over the holes made by pillars.
  8. Wing 8
    • For Ura CM/LM when the healers are off group you can use Breakrazor's Bastion as it has a low energy cost, so you can use it to help heal your allies (if needed). You will easily have a first phase of 32 to 33k, but DPS will fall off drastically if you must go off group for mechanics.
