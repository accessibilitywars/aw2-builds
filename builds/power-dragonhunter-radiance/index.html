Weapon Swap

Weapon swap is optional, all the available options will reduce DPS and lock you out of hammer, they should be prioritised based on utility requirements.

  1. Greatsword - Provides a long range, short cooldown leap and an AoE pull. Does decent burst damage with 4+2, but the sustained dps is far lower than hammer
  2. Bow - Bow provides a ranged option for bosses with melee DPS downtime, but can only reach 24k~ dps with this build.

Rotation and Utilities

Alternative Utilities

  • This build is heavily reliant on resolution 100% uptime, which can be reached with Hallowed Ground and "Save Yourselves!" alone. "Stand Your Ground!" also provides resolution, but not enough for 100% uptime unless your sub is providing enough to cover the 4~ second downtime. If your sub is providing 100% uptime on resolution, you can flex both slots, however its important to avoid anything with a cast time. Even the most damaging utility skills are a DPS loss compared to hammer 1.

  • Hallowed Ground provides pulsing group stability, and pulsing resolution, but results in a small DPS loss due to its cast time.

  • "Save Yourselves!" provides the all important resolution, among other boons, stuns breaks, and steals condis from allies.

  • "Stand Your Ground!" provides resolution, group stability, and group stun break.

  • "Advance!" for lots of extra group aegis

  • Smite Condition for a decent DPS boost, provided your sub has resolution uptime covered.

  • Judge's Intervention for boss fights that require you to travel alot. 1200-range gap-closer and stunbreak with a 20s cooldown.

Elite Skill

  • Signet of Courage is the default option. This should only ever be used as an emergency stun break or group stability. It has a long channel, but this can be interrupted at any point by simply moving.
  • "Feel My Wrath!" Can be nice for a little superspeed, and to cover a little quickness if you are separated from your boons.
  • Dragon's Maw For a little extra CC, and only a tiny DPS loss from its cast time.

DPS Rotation

  1. Opening - Hallowed Ground and "Save Yourselves!"
  2. Hammer Swing Hammer auto attack (1)
  3. Re-use the opening utilities off cooldown to upkeep resolution. If you notice allies are providing resolution, save Hallowed Ground for stability.

Crowd Control rotation

  • Zealot's Embrace (Hammer 3), Banish (Hammer 4), and Ring of Warding (Hammer 5) are all pretty good CC options.
  • Wings of Resolve (Traited F2) also provides immobilize.
  • Spear of Justice -> Hunter's Verdict will also do CC, however this should be your last CC option, as losing the justice passive temporarily may drop your DPS.
  • Sanctuary can be used in the flex slot for huge CC, if resolution uptime is covered by allies.

Sustainability

  • Your autos generate perma-protection.
  • Shield of Courage provides aegis, a stun break, as well as a decently long block duration that doesn't prevent you from attacking.
  • Use "Stand Your Ground!" for a group stun break and multiple stacks of stability.
