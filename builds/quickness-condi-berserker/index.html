Notes

This build relies on the Blademaster and Dual Wielding to commit to dual wielding swords with no weapon swap instead of the standard condition traits. Apart from being easier to play, the build has more regular access to the block skill has reduced cooldown to the block skill { skill(id="14400") }} (⚔5) which also contributes to some damage on actual fights.

You will need to use various skills to upkeep Berserk. If you drop out of Berserk, it can be very punishing. However, if perform the skills as required, the state can be sustained and the build becomes very fun and rewarding.

Rotation

Opener (optional)

  1. Head Butt → Berserk
  2. Scorched Earth (🏹Burst)(x2 ↔ Blood Reckoning)
  3. 🔁Weapon Swap -> Savage Leap (⚔2)
  • Your opener gives a significant amount of Quickness.
  • Blood Reckoning allows you to double cast Scorched Earth (🏹 Burst).
  • Savage Leap provides Fire Aura for extra DPS.
  • Performing/Repeating the opener will result in small DPS increases.

DPS Rotation

Use the following off-cooldown:

  1. Utilities
  2. Flaming Flurry (⚔Burst)(x2 ↔ Blood Reckoning)
  3. Sword Skills
    • Impale (⚔4)
    • Rend (⚔3)
  4. Bloodthirster (⚔Burst)
  • Use Flaming Flurry → Blood Reckoning together to double cast your Primal Burst.
  • Use Shattering Blow → Head Butt together to mitigate the stun effect.
  • Using Head Butt helps provide a buffer to upkeep Berserk status.
  • Outrage has no cast time and can be simultaneously casted together with other skills. DO NOT delay this skill.

Optimisations and CC.

  1. Smoldering Arrow (🏹 4) provides a small CC.
  2. Head Butt (Elite) is a strong CC skill.
  3. Use Bow Skills to upkeep adrenaline and DPS when locked out of range.
  4. Signet of Fury (when you need Adrenaline at range)
  5. A well timed Riposte (⚔5) increases damage significantly on actual fights.

Video

This bench is done with ascended gear, food, utilities and no infusions. The numbers are not grinded for and you should be able to easily surpass 30k DPS with optimisations.
